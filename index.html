More study space ideas

These are proposed additions based on the challenges students described.

Extra capacity during midterms

Consider opening or arranging additional designated study space during midterms and exam periods, when demand is higher. This would be separate from the regular library areas.

Study space between classes

Consider a dedicated study room near teaching blocks for students with gaps between lectures. It would be a study room—not an ordinary empty classroom—and could reduce time spent walking to the Central Library.

Both ideas are proposals for discussion, not claims that these spaces already exist.

Choose by study need

A quick guide based on the library arrangements described by students.

Need quiet, focused study?

Choose a Central Library study area. The library is expected to remain quiet, so it is best for individual study.

Need to talk or work together?

Use the basement for group discussion or practicals. For a discussion room in the Central Library, an appointment is required and the time limit is one hour.

Between classes: No nearby in-between study spot means a trip to the Central Library can eat into your break.

Library guide & known limitations

Information reflects the student-provided description of the library.

Level naming: The basement is Level 1; the first floor is Level 2; the second floor is Level 3. The website keeps the familiar floor names in its space cards to avoid confusion while the remaining level labels are confirmed.

Entry and exit: Turnstiles record entry and exit at the Central Library and the investment library.

Facilities: Charging pods, tables, and seats are available across the described study floors. The ground floor also has reception and washrooms.

Comfort note: Seating on the fourth and fifth floors was described as less comfortable than seating on the ground and second floors.

Not shown: Live occupancy, seat reservations, walking directions, opening hours, and investment-library floor details were not provided, so this prototype does not claim or simulate them.
